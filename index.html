Official Job Aid Reference Matrix

Summarized directly from pages 8 & 9 of Colourimetrics Job Aid.

# IF the X-Ray AND Then (Outcome)
1 Generated a system alarm (red overlay) on cremated remains of ANY volume. ETD trace does NOT alarm AND both CMT results are NEGATIVE. Clear the cremated remains.
2 Did NOT generate a system alarm (red overlay) BUT cremated remains are OVER 350 mL. ETD trace does NOT alarm AND both CMT results are NEGATIVE. Clear the cremated remains.
ETD trace alarms OR Both ETD & 1/both CMT results are POSITIVE. Follow ETD alarm resolution. Remains CANNOT be cleared.
ETD trace does NOT alarm BUT 1 or both CMT results are POSITIVE. Follow CMT alarm resolution. Remains CANNOT be cleared.
3 Generated a system alarm (red overlay) on cremated remains of any volume, OR Generated TWO system alarms (red and yellow). ETD trace does NOT alarm AND both CMT results are NEGATIVE. Remains CANNOT be cleared. Contact SDM/Line Lead.
ETD trace alarms OR Both ETD & 1/both CMT results are POSITIVE. Follow ETD alarm resolution. Remains CANNOT be cleared.
ETD trace does NOT alarm BUT 1 or both CMT results are POSITIVE. Follow CMT alarm resolution. Remains CANNOT be cleared.
4 Generated a shield alarm (yellow overlay). ETD/CMT is not required Remains CANNOT be cleared. Contact SDM/Line Lead.
